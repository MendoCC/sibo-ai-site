Delete my data

All your logging lives on your device. You delete everything by choosing Delete all my data in the app's privacy view, or by uninstalling the app.

If you also want the anonymous quota counters on our server removed, send an email to the address above. They contain no symptoms, no text and nothing that can be traced to you — but they should go if you ask.

Manage or cancel your subscription

The subscription belongs to the store you bought it from:

  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → SIBO AI Pro.
  • Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions.

The trial is the store's introductory offer and its length is shown in the store. If you cancel during the trial, you won't be charged.

Understanding the statistics

The app shows the strength of each connection together with its statistical uncertainty. A wide uncertainty interval means the evidence is thin — not that the connection is absent.

More days in the log give narrower intervals. That's why the app would rather say "more days needed" than give you a number you can't trust.

Does the app work offline?

Logging, the overview, the timeline and the pattern analysis all work with no connection at all — everything is calculated on the phone. The AI coach needs a network, because the answer comes from an external model.
